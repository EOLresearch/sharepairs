Introducing Simpatico: A Web-Based Social Connectivity Intervention for Grief

Researchers at Weill Cornell Medicine are enrolling participants in a research study to test Simpatico, a new online platform designed to connect people who are grieving through supportive conversation.

Participation Includes

  • Use the Simpatico web app for 2 weeks to chat with another participant who has experienced a similar loss
  • Completion of 3 Surveys:
    • Eligibility Screening
    • Baseline Survey
    • Post-Use Survey
  • Entirely remote
  • Receive a $60 Amazon gift card for completing all parts of the study

Study Eligibility

  • You have lost a loved one within the last 6 months – 10 years
  • You are 18 years old or older
  • You live in the U.S.
  • You are fluent in English
  • You have daily, at-home internet access
